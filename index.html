About Danish — Founder, SLATE Concept Studios

Hi, I'm Danish, founder of SLATE Concept Studios. With over a decade of experience in architectural services and visualization, I specialize in transforming ideas into spaces that are both functional and visually compelling.

At SLATE Concept Studios, we bridge the gap between concept and reality through thoughtful architectural design, detailed planning, and photorealistic visualization. Why choose SLATE Concept Studios

  • We start with how you live — routines, family size, storage, privacy and daylight come before elevations.
  • See it before you build it — 3D visualization explains design decisions so you can approve them with confidence.
  • We discuss your actual site — location, orientation, access and what we can support there.
  • Scope agreed in writing — deliverables, revisions, responsibilities and exclusions set out before appointment.

Design only, or design and execution

Design only — architectural planning, elevations or interior design. Drawings, specifications, visualization and revisions are defined in the proposal. You appoint the execution team unless we explicitly agree otherwise, and site visits or contractor coordination must be separately specified.

Turnkey design and execution — our responsibility runs from design through handover, within the agreed scope. We confirm execution availability for your site, and the proposal sets out materials, procurement, approvals, contractor coordination, site supervision, payment stages and exclusions.
